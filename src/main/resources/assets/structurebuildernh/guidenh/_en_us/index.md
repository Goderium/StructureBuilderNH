## Choose a site and build

- Use the terminal on a controller, on a block face to select the adjacent space, or in empty air to select your feet.
- Drag a controller from NEI into the top-left marker slot, or **Shift + left/right-click** a controller in your inventory.
- Set the channels and preview in the world if needed. Request ME crafting for missing materials or select **Start** when ready.

## World shortcuts

Sneak defaults to Shift.

- **Right-click** a preview or existing structure to open its task. Tap an editable controller or hatch marker to open Single Block Replacement.
- **Sneak + right-click** a structure to open its main task page, or use it in empty air to open Tasks.
- **Sneak + scroll** to cycle the material under the crosshair.
- **Ctrl + Sneak + right-click** to apply the current material to the whole group (matching symbols in the same structure piece).
- **Z** (default) deletes the current or last selected task and keeps placed blocks. It works on active previews and on completed or dismantled tasks. Finish typing first if the terminal interface is open.

## Move a controller preview

Hold right-click for 0.3 seconds to grab a movable preview, including distant controllers. Move or turn to reposition it and release right-click to confirm.

- **Hold right-click + scroll** adjusts distance by one block per notch.
- **Right-click → Sneak + scroll** turns horizontally in 90° steps.
- **Right-click → Ctrl + scroll** tilts in 90° steps.

Keep right-click held and then press the modifier. The first modifier wins until released. Release and press the other key again if it was already held.

## Interface shortcuts

- In **Browse model**, left-click blocks, left-drag to rotate, and scroll to zoom. In **Section**, use the axis button and layer slider, and left-drag the right-hand view to pan.
- Select a block and choose **Show in section**. Scroll or click a channel value to edit it. Tap right-click or press **Esc** to go back.
- In **Single block replacement**, hover over the marker slot and press a hotbar key to choose a block. Press **Enter** to place or replace, or **Esc** to go back.
